20.1 · Table se cube tak excel sheets ka dher

Pichhle lesson mein humne fact table dekha: (date, product, store) → revenue. Analyst ko is data ko alag-alag angle se dekhna hota hai: kabhi city-wise, kabhi quarter-wise, kabhi dono. Isko aasan banane ke liye data ko ek multidimensional array ki tarah dekhte hain jise data cube kehte hain.

Analogy: Ek Excel sheet mein rows = City, columns = Product, aur andar sales likhi hai — ye 2-D hua. Ab aisi hi sheet har quarter ki alag banao aur sab ko ek ke upar ek stack kar do — ye 3-D cube hua. Quarter = teesri dimension.

Ek cube se hum sirf poore (Time, City, Product) detail par hi nahi, kam dimensions par bhi summary maang sakte hain: sirf (Time, City) ke total, ya sirf Product ke total, ya poora grand total. Har aisi grouping ek alag cuboid hai.

Counting rule (no hierarchy)

n dimensions mein har dimension ke liye do choice: include karo ya ignore (ALL) karo. Isliye cuboids ki sankhya = 2 × 2 × ... × 2 (n baar) = 2n (base aur apex dono gin kar).

Cuboids ki sankhya
n (dimensions)12345610
Cuboids = 2n2481632641024

k dimensions wale cuboids = C(n, k). n = 4: 1 + 4 + 6 + 4 + 1 = 16 ✓.

Notation: n, k, C(n,k)

n = cube ki dimensions ki sankhya (hamare cube mein Time, City, Product: n = 3). k = group-by mein kitni dimensions rakhi (0 se n tak). C(n, k) = n mein se k dimensions chunne ke tareeke (“n choose k”).

Kaise gina? (n = 3)

  1. Har dimension ke liye 2 choice: rakho (group-by mein) ya hata do (ALL). Time: 2, City: 2, Product: 2.
  2. Total = 2 × 2 × 2 = 23 = 8 cuboids (upar wali table ki 8 rows).
  3. k ke hisaab se: k = 0: C(3,0) = 1 (apex); k = 1: C(3,1) = 3; k = 2: C(3,2) = 3; k = 3: C(3,3) = 1 (base). 1 + 3 + 3 + 1 = 8 ✓.

Cell ka ALL / * notation

Cuboid (Time, City) ke cell ko base cuboid ke terms mein likhte hain (Q1, Pune, *): * = “is dimension par ALL values ka total”. Hamare data mein (Q1, Pune, *) = 30 + 20 = 50. Apex cell = (*, *, *) = 480.

Kaise bani? (50 aur 480)

  1. (Q1, Pune, *): Q1 layer mein Pune ki row: Mobile 30, Laptop 20. Product ALL = dono jodo: 30 + 20 = 50.
  2. Apex (*, *, *): saari 12 cells jodo. Q1 layer = 60 + 40 + 30 + 20 + 45 + 25 = 220. Q2 layer = 70 + 50 + 35 + 25 + 50 + 30 = 260. Total = 220 + 260 = 480.
Lattice ka fayda

Neeche wala cuboid upar wale ko compute karwa sakta hai (roll-up). Jaise (City) ko (Time, City) se, ya (Time, City, Product) se bana lo. Isliye warehouse mein sab cuboids store karne ki zarurat nahi, kuch store karke baaki on-the-fly compute ho jate hain.

20.3 · Concept hierarchy ek dimension ke andar levels ki seedhi

Ab socho dimension Location ke andar khud kai levels hain: City → State → Country. Ek concept hierarchy low-level (specific) values ko high-level (general) values par map karti hai. Jaise Pune, Mumbai → Maharashtra; Maharashtra, Delhi → India.

Analogy: Google Maps: zoom-in karo to galli dikhti hai, zoom-out karo to sheher, phir rajya, phir desh. Har zoom-level = ek level.

Do alag cheezein mat milana

Concept hierarchy = ek dimension ke andar ke levels (City→State→Country). Cuboid lattice = kaun si dimensions group-by mein hain. Dono mil kar poora multi-level cube banate hain, jiska count agla section batata hai.

20.4 · Hierarchies ke saath cuboids ki sankhya GATE ka favourite

Agar har dimension mein koi hierarchy nahi, to har dimension ke 2 option (rakho / hatao) = 2n. Ab maan lo dimension i mein Li levels hain (jaise Time: Day, Month, Year to L = 3). Us dimension ko group-by mein ye Li levels mein se kisi ek par rakh sakte ho, ya poori tarah hata do (ALL). Matlab Li + 1 choices.

Time: DayMonthYearALL (hata do)= 3 + 1 = 4 choices
Formula
Total cuboids T = (L1 + 1) × (L2 + 1) × ... × (Ln + 1)

Yahan Li = dimension i ke actual levels, ALL ko chhod kar. Agar Li = 1 (koi hierarchy nahi), to factor = 2 ⇒ wapas 2n. Agar hierarchy lattice hai (Fig 20.4), to factor = us dimension ke distinct levels ki sankhya + 1 (ALL).

Notation: T, n, Li (2026 Q43 ke figure se)

n = dimensions ki sankhya. i = dimension ka number (1 se n). Li = dimension i ke hierarchy mein kitne levels hain (ALL ginti mein nahi). T = total cuboids. × = gunaa (product).

2026 Q43 ke figure mein: n = 3. Dimension 1 = Time, L1 = 3 (Year, Month, Day). Dimension 2 = Location, L2 = 3 (Country, State, City). Dimension 3 = Product, L3 = 3 (Item Category, Item Type, Item).

Q43 par formula lagao

  1. Time ko group-by mein 4 tareeke se rakh sakte ho: Year, Month, Day, ya ALL (hata do). Isliye L1 + 1 = 3 + 1 = 4 choices. Location ke bhi 4 (Country / State / City / ALL), Product ke bhi 4.
  2. Har cuboid = teeno dimensions mein ek-ek choice. Jaise (Year, Country, Item Category) ek cuboid; (Month, ALL, Item) doosra; (Day, City, Item) = base cuboid; (ALL, ALL, ALL) = apex.
  3. T = (L1+1)(L2+1)(L3+1) = 4 × 4 × 4 = 43 = 64.

Worked examples

Example 1 — 3 dimensions, 3 levels har ek

Time (Day, Month, Year), Location (City, State, Country), Product (Item, Type, Category). T = 4 × 4 × 4 = 64.

Example 2 — alag-alag levels

Time: 3 levels, Location: 2 levels, Product: 1 level, Customer: 1 level. T = 4 × 3 × 2 × 2 = 48.

Kaise?

  1. Time (L = 3): choices Day / Month / Year / ALL = 4.
  2. Location (L = 2): choices City / Country / ALL (maan lo 2 levels City, Country) = 3.
  3. Product (L = 1): choices Item / ALL = 2. Customer (L = 1): 2.
  4. Gunaa: 4 × 3 × 2 × 2 = 48.

Example 3 — lattice wali Time

Time ke levels {Day, Week, Month, Quarter, Year} = 5 (+ALL = 6). Location chain 3 levels (+ALL = 4). T = 6 × 4 = 24.

Kaise?

  1. Time ke 5 distinct levels (Day, Week, Month, Quarter, Year) + ALL = 6 choices (lattice mein bhi har level ek alag choice hai).
  2. Location: City / State / Country + ALL = 4 choices.
  3. 6 × 4 = 24.

Example 4 — chhote case ko haath se ginke verify karo

2 dimensions: A (levels A1 > A2) aur B (single level B1). Choices for A = {A1, A2, ALL}; for B = {B1, ALL}. Total 3 × 2 = 6: (A1,B1), (A2,B1), (ALL,B1), (A1,ALL), (A2,ALL), (ALL,ALL). Formula (2+1)(1+1) = 6 ✓.

Trap

Level ginte waqt ALL ko Li mein mat gino, warna +1 do baar lag jayega. Aur “4 levels (including ALL)” likha ho to factor seedha 4 hai, +1 nahi. Question ka wording dhyan se padho.

Ab tum try karo

Q. 5 dimensions; Time ke 4 levels, baaki 4 ke 2-2 levels. Cuboids?

Ans: (4+1) × (2+1)4 = 5 × 81 = 405.

Kaise bani? (dice)

  1. Teeno conditions apply karo: City sirf Delhi ya Mumbai (Pune hata), Product sirf Mobile (Laptop hata), Quarter Q1 ya Q2 (dono rakhe).
  2. Bachi cells copy: (Q1, Delhi, Mobile) = 60; (Q1, Mumbai, Mobile) = 45; (Q2, Delhi, Mobile) = 70; (Q2, Mumbai, Mobile) = 50.

20.6 · SQL mein CUBE aur ROLLUP GROUP BY ke extensions

Har cuboid ke liye alag GROUP BY query likhne ke bajay SQL mein ek hi query mein kai groupings maangne ka tareeka hai.

Kaise bani? (CUBE ke 4 tables)

  1. (D1, D2): har (D1, D2) combination ek group, group mein ek hi row; isliye sum = val khud: (a,x) 1, (a,y) 2, ... 6 rows.
  2. (D1): D2 ignore, D1 ke hisaab se group: a = 1 + 2 = 3; b = 3 + 4 = 7; c = 5 + 6 = 11. D2 column mein NULL (matlab “ALL”).
  3. (D2): D1 ignore: x = 1 + 3 + 5 = 9; y = 2 + 4 + 6 = 12. D1 column NULL.
  4. (): koi grouping nahi, poora table ek group: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 21. Dono columns NULL.
  5. Chaaron tables ko UNION: 6 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 12 rows.
Row-count formulas
  • Grouping sets in CUBE(n columns) = 2n (ye rows nahi, groupings hain).
  • Agar sab combinations present hain aur di = distinct values of column i:
    rows in CUBE = Π(di + 1). Example: (3+1)(2+1) = 12.
  • ROLLUP(C1, C2, ..., Cn) sirf prefix groupings deta hai: (C1..Cn), (C1..Cn-1), ..., (C1), (). Matlab n + 1 grouping sets.
    Rows = d1d2...dn + d1...dn-1 + ... + d1 + 1.

ROLLUP(D1, D2) mere table par: (D1,D2) = 6, (D1) = 3, () = 1 ⇒ 10 rows. Par ROLLUP(D2, D1): (D2,D1) = 6, (D2) = 2, () = 1 ⇒ 9 rows. Order matter karta hai ROLLUP mein, CUBE mein nahi (CUBE sab subsets deta hai).

Kaise bani? (ROLLUP ki rows)

  1. ROLLUP(D1, D2) = prefix groupings: (D1, D2), (D1), (). Rows: 6 (D1, D2 ke 3 × 2 combos) + 3 (D1 ki values a, b, c) + 1 (grand) = 10.
  2. ROLLUP(D2, D1) = (D2, D1), (D2), (). Rows: 6 + 2 (D2 ki values x, y) + 1 = 9.
  3. Notation: di = column i ki distinct values ki sankhya (D1: d1 = 3, D2: d2 = 2). Π = gunaa. CUBE rows = (d1+1)(d2+1) = 4 × 3 = 12.
GROUP BY ROLLUP(Year, Quarter, Month)   -- (Y,Q,M), (Y,Q), (Y), ()   : hierarchy ke liye natural
GROUP BY GROUPING SETS ((D1), (D2))     -- sirf yahi do groupings, aur kuch nahi
Traps
  • CUBE ke output rows 2n nahi; wo to grouping sets hain. Rows = Π(d+1).
  • Agar data mein khud NULL values hain, to subtotal ke NULL aur data ke NULL mein confusion hoti hai (isliye GROUPING() function hota hai). Question mein “non-NULL” likha ho to formula chalega.
  • Agar sab combinations present nahi hain (sparse data), to rows formula se kam hongi.

20.7 · Query kis cuboid se answer hogi? kam important

Query ek cuboid X par hai. Wo kisi bhi cuboid Y se compute ho sakti hai jo X se zyada ya barabar detailed ho (lattice mein X ke neeche, connected). Kuch cuboids hi store hon (partial) to sabse chhota aisa Y chuna jata hai (kam rows scan).

Counting trick

n dimensions (no hierarchy) mein, k dimensions wale cuboid X ko answer karne wale cuboids (khud X ko milake) = 2n−k. Example n = 3, query (Product) k = 1: (Product), (T,P), (C,P), (T,C,P) = 4 = 22 ✓.

Syllabus mein nahi — skip

Materialization strategies, ROLAP / MOLAP / HOLAP, sparse-cube storage, iceberg cube: GATE DA syllabus ke bahar.

GATE DA 2026 · Q18 (1 mark, MCQ) — OLAP operation

Consider that the visualization of a 3-dimensional data cube is showing Sales Quantity for each combination of the attributes Product Type, Month and Country. From this, if we want to further visualize the Sales Quantity for each combination of Product Type, Month and State, which of the following OLAP operations should be performed?
(A) Slicing   (B) Dicing   (C) Roll-up   (D) Drill-down

  1. Pehle dimensions: (Product Type, Month, Country). Baad mein: (Product Type, Month, State). Sirf Location dimension ka level badla.
  2. Hierarchy: Country (top) → State → City. Country se State = zyada detail (ek country mein kai states).
  3. Zyada detail = drill-down. Slicing/dicing mein level nahi badalta, sirf values filter hoti hain; roll-up ulta hota (State → Country).
Answer (derived):
✔ (D) Drill-down

GATE DA 2025 · Q46 (2 marks, MCQ) — GROUP BY CUBE row count

Consider a fact table in an OLAP application: Facts(D1, D2, val), where D1 and D2 are its dimension attributes and val is a dependent attribute. Suppose attribute D1 takes 3 values and D2 takes 2 values, and all combinations of these values are present in the table Facts. How many tuples are there in the result of the following query?

SELECT D1, D2, sum(val)
FROM Facts
GROUP BY CUBE (D1, D2);

(A) 1   (B) 6   (C) 9   (D) 12

  1. CUBE(D1, D2) = 4 grouping sets: (D1,D2), (D1), (D2), ().
  2. (D1,D2): 3 × 2 = 6 rows (sab combinations present). (D1): 3. (D2): 2. (): 1.
  3. Total = 6 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 12. Shortcut: (3+1)(2+1) = 12 (Section 20.6 ka tableau dekho). Official answer key paper PDF mein nahi hai; ye derived hai.
Answer (derived):
✔ (D) 12
Trap: 4 = number of grouping sets (not rows); 6 = sirf detailed rows; 9 = 6 + 3, yaani (D2) aur () ke rows bhool gaye.

1. 5 dimensions, kisi mein hierarchy nahi. Total cuboids (base aur apex milake)?

2. 3 dimensions jinke levels (ALL ke bina) 2, 3 aur 4 hain. Total cuboids?
✔ (2+1)(3+1)(4+1) = 3 × 4 × 5 = 60
3. 4 dimensions (no hierarchy). 2-dimensional cuboids kitne? 3-dimensional?
✔ C(4,2) = 6; C(4,3) = 4
Total 1+4+6+4+1 = 16.
4. Month → Quarter par jaana: roll-up ya drill-down?
✔ Roll-up
Month specific, Quarter general.
5. “Sirf Delhi ka sales, sab products aur quarters” = ?
✔ Slice (City = Delhi)
Ek dimension, ek value.
6. “Q1 ya Q2, aur city Delhi ya Pune, sab products” = ?
✔ Dice
Do dimensions par selection (Quarter aur City).
7. Pivot ke baad cells ki values badalti hain?
✔ Nahi, sirf orientation (rows/columns) badalta hai.
8. Time ke levels: Day, Week, Month, Quarter, Year (Day→Week; Day→Month→Quarter→Year). Location: City, State, Country. Total cuboids?
✔ (5+1)(3+1) = 6 × 4 = 24
Lattice mein har distinct level ek choice hai.
9. CUBE(A, B, C) mein d = 2, 3, 4 (sab combinations present). Rows?
✔ (2+1)(3+1)(4+1) = 60
Grouping sets = 8; rows = 60.
10. Same data par ROLLUP(A, B, C) ke rows? Aur ROLLUP(C, B, A) ke rows?
✔ ROLLUP(A,B,C) = 24 + 6 + 2 + 1 = 33. ROLLUP(C,B,A) = 24 + 12 + 4 + 1 = 41
Pehle column se prefix bante hain, isliye order matter karta hai.
11. 4 dimensions (no hierarchy) mein sirf (B) cuboid ko kitne cuboids answer kar sakte hain (khud milake)?
✔ 24−1 = 8
B wale sab: B, AB, BC, BD, ABC, ABD, BCD, ABCD = 8.
12. Query (Product Type, Month, Country) se (Product Type, Quarter, Country) par jana. Kaunsa operation?
✔ Roll-up (Month → Quarter)
Time dimension mein level upar gaya.
13. Q1 ka Pune Mobile = 30 aur Q2 ka 35. Year roll-up par Pune Mobile?
✔ 65
SUM aggregate hai, isliye 30 + 35.
Lesson 20 cheat box
  • Cell = ek value per dimension (ya *). Base cuboid = sabhi n dimensions; apex = () = 1 cell grand total.
  • Cuboids = 2n (no hierarchy). With hierarchies = Π(Li + 1) (L ALL ke bina; lattice mein distinct levels + ALL).
  • 2026 Q43 = 43 = 64.
  • Roll-up = upar/summary. Drill-down = neeche/detail (Country → State = drill-down, 2026 Q18). Slice = 1 dim fixed. Dice = kai dim conditions. Pivot = rotate.
  • CUBE: grouping sets = 2n; rows = Π(di + 1) (full data). 2025 Q46 = 12. ROLLUP: prefix sets, rows = d1 + d1d2 + ... + 1; order matters.
  • Query on k-dim cuboid ko 2n−k cuboids answer kar sakte hain.
