Ek line mein farq (ye lesson isi par tika hai)

Cardinality = “zyada se zyada kitne?” (maximum: 1 ya N).   Participation = “kam se kam ek zaroori hai ya nahi?” (minimum: 0 ya 1). Dono alag facts hain — ek-doosre se nahi nikalte.

2.1 · Relationship — kaun kisse jura hai instance & set

Do entity types ke beech ka meaningful rishta = relationship type (diamond). Jaise STUDENT ENROLLS COURSE. Actual jode (Rahul–DBMS) relationship instances hain; unka poora collection relationship set.

Fig 2.1 — ENROLLS ke 5 relationship instances (5 lines)
ShabdMatlab
Relationship typeENROLLS (diamond) — structure
Relationship instance(Rahul, DBMS) — ek jodi / ek line
Relationship setAbhi ki saari jodiyan = 5 pairs

Mathematical roop (GATE yehi poochta hai)

Agar entity sets E1, E2 hain, to relationship set R unke cross product ka subset hai:

R ⊆ E1 × E2     (ternary mein: R ⊆ E1 × E2 × E3)

Matlab: har instance ek tuple (e1, e2) hai. Agar |E1|=3 aur |E2|=3 to sabse zyada 3×3 = 9 instances ho sakte hain (jo sirf M:N mein mumkin hai). Cardinality constraints is subset ko chhota kar dete hain.

Trap: ek hi jodi do baar nahi

Relationship set ek set hai — (Rahul, DBMS) do baar nahi aa sakti. Agar Rahul DBMS kai baar padh sakta hai (alag semesters), to ya to Semester ko ek aur entity banao (ternary) ya relationship ko hi entity bana do.

Ek jodi ke beech kai relationship bhi ho sakte hain

EMPLOYEE aur DEPARTMENT ke beech do relationships ho sakte hain: WORKS_FOR (N:1) aur MANAGES (1:1). Ye alag-alag diamonds banenge — ek-doosre se independent.

2.2 · Degree — kitne entity types jude hain? unary, binary, ternary

Degree of a relationship type = us relationship mein participate karne wale entity types ki ginti.

Unary (degree 1) / recursive: ek hi entity type, do roles
Ternary (degree 3): ek saath teen. (Binary, degree 2, jaise STUDENT–ENROLLS–COURSE, aap 2.1 mein dekh chuke ho.)
  • Unary / recursive: EMPLOYEE supervises EMPLOYEE. Entity type ek hi hai, par wo do alag roles mein aati hai (boss aur subordinate) — isliye role names likhna zaroori.
  • Binary: STUDENT enrolls COURSE.
  • Ternary: SUPPLIER supplies PART to PROJECT (teeno ek hi fact mein).
  • N-ary: 4 ya zyada — rare.
Trap: degree ko galat cheezon se mat naapo

Degree ≠ rows ki ginti, ≠ columns ki ginti, ≠ logon ki ginti. Entity types ki ginti hai. Sirf ek table ka degree “columns” hota hai (Lesson 4) — ER relationship ke degree se alag cheez.

ExampleDegreeKyun?
EMPLOYEE works_on PROJECT2 (binary)Do entity types
PERSON is_married_to PERSON1 (unary)Ek entity type, do roles (husband/wife)
DOCTOR prescribes MEDICINE to PATIENT3 (ternary)Teen entity types
COURSE has_prerequisite COURSE1 (unary)Recursive, M:N
Ab tum try karo

Q. “Teacher teaches a course to a class” ka degree? Aur “Employee manages Employee” ka?

Answer: Pehla = 3 (TEACHER, COURSE, CLASS). Doosra = 1 (unary: ek hi entity type). Kuch books recursive ko “do roles” kehti hain par degree entity types se hi ginti hai.

2.3 · Cardinality ratio — “zyada se zyada kitne?” 1:1, 1:N, M:N

Ek entity maximum kitni entities se juri ho sakti hai — ye cardinality ratio batata hai. Teen hi shapes hote hain (N:1 sirf 1:N ka ulta naam hai):

1:1 — ek-ek jodi

Har E1 se at most ek E2, aur har E2 se at most ek E1. e3 ka koi partner nahi — allowed (participation partial).

1:N — ek, kai

Ek DEPT ke kai EMP, par har EMP ka at most ek DEPT. d3 khaali, e-side mein koi do DEPT se nahi.

M:N — kai-kai

  1. Look-across rule (Chen): line par jo number/letter likha hai wo doosri taraf ki entity ke baare mein bolta hai. DEPARTMENT —1— WORKS_FOR —N— EMPLOYEE padho: “ek department ke N employees” (N EMPLOYEE ki taraf), “ek employee ka 1 department”.
  2. Check karne ka tarika: har entity se pucho “ye zyada se zyada kitni doosri side ki entities se juri ho sakti hai?” 1 ya >1. Dono answers se ratio bana lo.
  3. N ka matlab: koi bhi number (zero se upar ki sankhya), koi fixed number nahi. N aur M alag letters sirf ye dikhane ke liye ki dono side ke “kai” ek hi number zaroori nahi.
RatioA sideB sideReal exampleTable mein (Lesson 3)
1:1≤ 1 B≤ 1 APERSON – PASSPORT; EMPLOYEE manages DEPARTMENTEk side mein doosri ki FK (UNIQUE)
1:Nkai B≤ 1 ADEPARTMENT – EMPLOYEE; MOTHER – CHILDN-side mein FK
N:1≤ 1 Bkai AEMPLOYEE – DEPARTMENT (same as 1:N ulta)N-side (A) mein FK
M:Nkai Bkai ASTUDENT – COURSE; ACTOR – MOVIENayi table (dono keys)
Trap: “kya N ka matlab N rows hona zaroori hai?”

Nahi. 1:N mein N sirf maximum limit hai: ek department ke 0, 1 ya 500 employees ho sakte hain. Cardinality schema ka rule hai, instance ki ginti nahi. Aur ye bhi: ek instance (data) dekh kar aap cardinality prove nahi kar sakte — sirf disprove (galat sabit) kar sakte ho (Section 2.8).

Ab tum try karo

Q. “Ek student ek hi mentor se juda hai; ek mentor kai students ka hai.” MENTOR : STUDENT ratio?

Answer: 1:N (MENTOR 1, STUDENT N). Table mein FK STUDENT table mein MentorID.

2.4 · Participation kam se kam ek hona zaroori hai? total vs partial

Cardinality ne maximum bataya. Ab minimum: kya har entity ka kisi relationship mein hona zaroori hai?

TypeMatlabMinimumDiagram
Total (mandatory / existence dependency)Har entity at least ek relationship instance mein zaroor aaye1Double line
Partial (optional)Kuch entities kisi mein na bhi aayein0Single line

Ek hi relationship, teen alag participation rules

(a) Dono partial
(b) EMPLOYEE total, DEPT partial
(c) Dono total

Diagram (b) ki reading: double line EMPLOYEE se hai → har employee ka kisi department mein hona zaroori hai (koi bekaar/bina-dept employee nahi). DEPARTMENT par single line → department khaali ho sakta hai (abhi koi employee nahi). Ye sab ek hi tarah ki cardinality (1:N) ke saath possible hai — isliye participation aur cardinality independent hain.

Real life mein kaise sochein?
  • “Har account kisi customer ka hona hi chahiye” → ACCOUNT total.
  • “Customer bina account ke bhi ho sakta hai” → CUSTOMER partial.
  • “Order bina customer ke nahi hota” → ORDER total.
Traps
  • Total participation extra table nahi banata — ye NOT NULL / constraint ban kar FK par lagta hai. (2026 Q61 ka same trap.)
  • Total = min 1 (kam se kam ek) ≠ “exactly one”. Exactly one = total aur max 1.
  • Weak entity hamesha total participate karti hai identifying relationship mein (Lesson 3).
Ab tum try karo

Q. “Department empty ho sakta hai; har employee exactly ek department mein.” Cardinality aur participation?

Answer: DEPT:EMP = 1:N; EMPLOYEE total; DEPARTMENT partial. (Min-max mein: Employee (1,1), Department (0,N).)

2.5 · (min, max) notation — dono baatein ek saath structural constraint

Cardinality (max) aur participation (min) ko ek jodi mein likh sakte hain: (min, max), har entity ki line ke saath. Ye ek entity ko ek relationship mein kam se kam aur zyada se zyada kitni baar aane ki ijaazat hai, wo batata hai.

Fig 2.2 — (min, max): har DEPARTMENT 0..N baar, har EMPLOYEE bilkul 1 baar WORKS_FOR mein
(min, max)MatlabParticipationCardinality
(0, 1)Ho bhi sakta hai, ho to ek hi baarPartialek
(1, 1)Bilkul ek baarTotalek
(0, N)Zero ya kaiPartialkai
(1, N)Kam se kam ek, kai ho sakte hainTotalkai
Dono notations ka jodna

Diagram: EMPLOYEE (1,1) — WORKS_FOR — (0,N) DEPARTMENT. Is se:
• Employee side ka max = 1 ⇒ ek employee ke ek department ⇒ DEPT:EMP mein 1 DEPT ki taraf.
• Department side ka max = N ⇒ ek dept ke kai employees ⇒ EMP ki taraf N. Ratio 1:N.
• Employee min = 1 ⇒ EMPLOYEE total. Dept min = 0 ⇒ partial.

Trap: kaun si book kaunsi taraf likhti hai

Elmasri–Navathe (GATE mein common): (min, max) us entity ki line par, us entity ke paas likhte hain — “ye entity kitni baar aati hai”. Chen ka 1/N ratio look-across hota hai (doosri entity ke baare mein). Kuch books (UML/crow's foot) opposite tarah se likhti hain. Diagram dekhte waqt hamesha pehle legend/question ki wording dekho; wording-based sawaal (“har employee exactly ek dept”) mein koi confusion nahi.

Ab tum try karo

Q. EMPLOYEE (0,1) — MANAGES — (1,1) DEPARTMENT. Matlab? Ratio?

Answer: Har department ka exactly ek manager (DEPARTMENT total); ek employee kisi bhi department ka manager ho bhi sakta hai, nahi bhi; ho to ek hi. Ratio 1:1.

2.6 · Relationship ke attributes kis par depend karta hai?

Kuch information na to ek entity ki hoti hai na dusri ki, balki jodi ki hoti hai. Rahul ne DBMS 10 Jan ko join kiya aur Python 15 Jan ko — EnrollDate na to sirf STUDENT ki property hai, na sirf COURSE ki.

Fig 2.3 — EnrollDate relationship ENROLLS ka attribute (diamond se juda oval)

Attribute ko kahan shift kar sakte ho?

RatioRelationship attribute ko entity mein shift?Kyun?
1:1Haan, kisi bhi ek entity meinHar entity ka ek hi partner hai
1:NHaan, sirf N-side entity meinN-side ki ek entity ka ek hi partner hai → value ek hi banti hai. 1-side ke paas kai values ban jaayengi
M:NNahi — relationship ke saath hi rahegaDono taraf kai partner; value jodi par depend karti hai

Example: EMPLOYEE (N) works_for DEPARTMENT (1), attribute StartDate → EMPLOYEE.StartDate bana sakte ho (har employee ki ek hi StartDate). Par ENROLLS (M:N) ka EnrollDate kisi entity mein nahi rakh sakte — Rahul ki kaunsi date? DBMS wali ya Python wali?

Trap: ER attribute ≠ foreign key

ER mein relationship attribute aur table mein FK alag cheezein hain. EnrollDate = relationship attribute (ER level). StudentID, CourseID = foreign keys (table level, Lesson 3). Bridge table ENROLL(StudentID, CourseID, EnrollDate) mein teeno aate hain.

2.7 · Ternary relationship teen entity types ek saath

Fig 2.4 — SUPPLIES: M:N:P, attribute Quantity
Fig 2.5 — ASSIGNED: TEACHER side = 1 (har student-course pair ka ek teacher)

Cardinality ka matlab ternary mein

Teen-way relationship mein ek leg par 1 ka matlab: baaki do entities ki fixed jodi ke liye us entity ki at most ek value. Fig 2.5: ek (STUDENT, COURSE) jodi ke liye at most ek TEACHER. Fig 2.4 mein koi leg 1 nahi — M:N:P.

Ternary ko 3 binary se replace karna safe nahi

Maan lo asli triples (supplier, part, project) ye hain: (S1,P1,J2), (S1,P2,J1), (S2,P1,J1). Teen binary relations bana do:

Ab inhe wapas jodo: (S1, P1, J1) bhi nikalta hai — kyunki S1–P1, S1–J1 aur P1–J1 teeno sach hain. Par ye triple asli data mein nahi tha! Yani 3 binary mein information kho gayi (spurious tuple).

Rule

Jab fact teeno ka ek saath ho, ternary hi rakho. Sirf tab 3 binary se badal sakte ho jab koi constraint ho jo triple ko binary facts se banne deta ho (jaise 1-leg wala functional rule) — GATE mein default: ternary ≠ 3 binary.

Ternary ka key kya hoga? (Lesson 3 mein table banate waqt)

Do legs 1 (N : 1 : 1)Sirf N-side entity ki key
Ab tum try karo

Q. ASSIGNED(Teacher 1, Student N, Course M): bridge table ASSIGNED ki primary key?

Answer: (StudentID, CourseID); TeacherID sirf foreign key (non-key attribute).

2.8 · Counting: kitne relationship instances? GATE ka favourite numerical

A. Data dekh kar cardinality nikalna

Maan lo R ke instances ye hain: (a1,b1), (a2,b1), (a3,b2).

  1. Har A kitne B se juda? a1→1, a2→1, a3→1. Kabhi >1 nahi.
  2. Har B kitne A se juda? b1→2 (a1,a2), b2→1. >1 hai.
  3. Is instance ke hisaab se A:B = N:1 (B ka ek, A ke kai) — ya koi bhi weaker ya zyada permissive constraint, e.g. M:N.
Instance se cardinality sirf disprove hoti hai

Upar ke data se hum keh sakte hain ki 1:1 nahi ho sakta (b1 ke do partners hain) aur 1:N (A one-side) bhi nahi ho sakta. Par ye N:1 hi hai, pakka nahi — M:N bhi consistent hai. GATE sawaal aksar yehi poochta hai: “in options mein kaunsi cardinality nahi ho sakti?”

B. Maximum / minimum instances (sizes diye hon)

|E1| = m, |E2| = n. Instance = ek line.

ConstraintMaximum instancesMinimum instances
M:N, dono partialm × n0
M:N, dono totalm × nmax(m, n) (har entity kam se kam ek line)
1:1, partialmin(m, n)0
1:1, dono totalm = n zaroori; instances = mm (same)
1:N (E1 = one-side), partialn (har E2 at most ek E1 se)0
1:N, N-side (E2) totalnn (har E2 ke tarah exactly ek line)
1:N, one-side (E1) totalnm (har E1 ko kam se kam ek E2 — aur ye alag hoon, isliye m ≤ n zaroori)
Seedha formula yaad

Max = (har entity ki apni limit se banne wale lines ka) minimum. 1:N mein N-side ki har entity at most 1 line deti hai, to max = |N-side|. M:N mein koi limit nahi: m×n.    Min = total participation wali entity sets ki sabse badi ginti (har ek ko line chahiye, ek line do entities cover kar sakti hai).

C. Ternary ka maximum

M:N:P → |A|·|B|·|C|. Agar C-leg par 1 hai (har (A,B) jodi ke liye at most ek C): max = |A|·|B|. Agar A-leg par 1 hai: max = |B|·|C|. (Jis leg par 1 hai, wo product mein nahi aati.)

2.9 · English sentence → ER cardinality translate karne ka recipe

  1. Sentence ke do nouns (entities) aur verb (relationship) pakdo.
  2. Pehle E1 → E2 padho: “ek E1 ke kitne E2?” (≤1 ya >1) → max on E2 side.
  3. Phir E2 → E1: “ek E2 ke kitne E1?” → max on E1 side.
  4. “Har / every / must / at least one” = total. “May / can / optionally” = partial.
  5. “exactly one” = total + max 1 = (1,1).
SentenceRatioParticipation
Har employee exactly ek department mein; department mein kai employeesDEPT : EMP = 1:NEMP total; DEPT partial (agar “empty ho sakta hai” likha ho)
Ek person ke at most ek passport; har passport kisi ek person kaPERSON : PASSPORT = 1:1PASSPORT total; PERSON partial
Student kai courses le sakta hai; course mein kai studentsM:NDono partial (default)
Har professor kam se kam ek course padhata hai; course ek hi professor padhata haiPROF : COURSE = 1:NPROF total; COURSE total (agar “har course ka ek prof”)
Ek customer kai orders; har order exactly ek customer kaCUST : ORDER = 1:NORDER total
Dekho: PERSON–PASSPORT: double line PASSPORT ki taraf (har passport ek person ka)
Classic mistake

“Ek department mein kai employees” sunke log DEPARTMENT par N likh dete hain. Look-across rule yaad rakho: 1:N mein 1 DEPT par, N EMP par (kaun “kai” hai wo N). Ulta likha to table mein FK galat jagah jaayegi.

2.10 · GATE-style numerical solved step by step

N1. STUDENT (50 rows) aur COURSE (10 rows), ENROLLS M:N, dono total participation. Minimum aur maximum relationship instances?
✔ Min = 50, Max = 500
Min = max(50, 10) = 50: 50 students ko kam se kam ek line chahiye; ye 50 lines 10 courses ko bhi cover kar sakti hain (har course ko ≥1 student). Max = 50×10 = 500.
N2. DEPARTMENT (8) 1:N EMPLOYEE (120). EMPLOYEE total, DEPARTMENT partial. Instances kitne? Aur kitne departments khaali ho sakte hain (max)?
✔ Instances = 120; khaali departments max = 7
Har employee exactly ek line → 120. 120 employees kam se kam ek department mein jaayenge, to kam se kam 1 dept non-empty → max 7 khaali.
N3. 1:1 relationship between A (5 entities, total) aur B (8 entities, partial). Kitne instances? B ke kitne entities unmatched?
✔ Instances = 5; unmatched B = 3
A total aur 1:1: har A exactly ek B se; B distinct (kyunki ek B ke at most ek A). To 5 lines, 5 B matched, 3 unmatched.
N4. Ternary R(A,B,C) with |A|=3, |B|=4, |C|=5. C-leg par 1 hai (har (A,B) pair ke liye at most ek C). Max instances?
✔ 12
Har (A,B) jodi ek se zyada line nahi de sakti: 3×4 = 12 jodiyan, har ek ≤ 1 line.
N5. Same ternary lekin A-leg par 1 (har (B,C) pair ke liye at most ek A). Max?
✔ 20
(B,C) jodiyan = 4×5 = 20, har ek ≤ 1 line.
N6. 1:N, DEPT (3) one-side total, EMP (20) partial. Min aur max instances?
✔ Min 3, Max 20
Har dept ko kam se kam ek emp chahiye aur emp distinct hone chahiye (ek emp ke at most ek dept) → min 3. Max: har emp at most ek line → 20.

Par DA 2026 Q61 (Lesson 3.10) ka statement hai “Let R12 be a many-to-many relationship between E1 and E2. Participation of both E1 and E2 in R12 is total” — yaani is lesson ki bhasha ko samajhna us sawaal ko solve karne ke liye zaroori hai: M:N → bridge table; total participation → koi extra table nahi, bas constraint.

2.12 · Practice questions pehle khud, phir answer

P1. Relationship set R ⊆ E1 × E2. Agar |E1|=4, |E2|=6, to R mein kitne instances sambhav (max) agar relationship M:N hai? Agar 1:1 hai?
✔ M:N: 24; 1:1: 4
1:1 mein har E1 ke at most ek partner → max min(4,6)=4.
P2. “Ek mother ke kai bachche; har bachche ki ek hi mother” MOTHER:CHILD? FK kahan?
✔ 1:N; FK CHILD table mein (MotherID)
N-side mein FK.
P3. (min,max) diagram: STUDENT (0,N) — ENROLLS — (1,N) COURSE. Student partial hai ya total? Course?
✔ STUDENT partial (min 0); COURSE total (min 1)
Har course mein kam se kam ek student; student bina course ke bhi ho sakta hai.
P4. Ek relationship mein EMPLOYEE (1,1) aur PROJECT (1,N) hai. Kya EMPLOYEE bina PROJECT ke ho sakta hai?
✔ Nahi (min = 1)
EMPLOYEE ka (1,1): exactly ek project mein hona zaroori. PROJECT ka (1,N): har project mein kam se kam ek employee.
P5. Ternary R(A,B,C) M:N:P, sizes 2,3,4. Max instances? Minimum agar sab total?
✔ Max 24; min 4
2×3×4=24. Min: har entity ko ≥1 line; 4 lines se C ke saare 4 cover hote hain, aur A (2), B (3) bhi cover ho sakte hain → max(2,3,4) = 4.
P6. 1:1 relationship A(6, total) — B(6, total). Kitne instances? Agar B ki ginti 7 ho aur dono total to?
✔ 6. B=7 hone par dono total ek saath impossible
1:1 + dono total ⇒ |A| = |B|.
P7. Kaunsa sahi hai: (A) 1:N mein N side par FK (B) M:N mein bridge table (C) dono (D) koi nahi.
✔ (C)
Lesson 3 ke rules ka preview.
P8. Attribute “grade” STUDENT–COURSE (M:N) relationship ka attribute hai. Isse STUDENT table mein shift kar sakte hain?
✔ Nahi
Ek student ke kai courses, har ek ka alag grade.
P9. Instance data: (x1,y1),(x1,y2),(x2,y1). Kya R 1:1 ho sakta hai? 1:N (X one-side)? N:1? M:N?
✔ 1:1 nahi; 1:N (X one) nahi (y1 ke 2 X); N:1 nahi (x1 ke 2 Y); M:N hi consistent
x1 do Y se; y1 do X se — dono taraf kai.
P10. “SUPERVISES” (EMPLOYEE, unary) 1:N. Roles kaun? Degree?
✔ Roles: Supervisor (1) aur Subordinate (N); degree 1 (unary)
Table: EMPLOYEE(EmpID, …, SupervisorID FK → EmpID).
P11. True/False: Total participation ka matlab hai ki relationship 1:1 ya 1:N hona zaroori hai.
✔ False
M:N bhi total ho sakta hai (Q61).
P12. Ek relationship instance (e1, e2) ka alag copy (e1, e2) kya set mein ek aur baar aa sakta hai?
✔ Nahi
Relationship set ⊆ E1×E2 ek set hai; duplicate nahi.

2.13 · Lesson 2 cheat box last-night revision

Do alag sawaal
  • Cardinality = max (1 ya N), look-across
  • Participation = min (0 = partial, 1 = total), double line
  • (min,max): (0,1) (1,1) (0,N) (1,N)
  • Degree = # entity types: unary 1, binary 2, ternary 3
Numerics
  • M:N max = m·n; min (both total) = max(m,n)
  • 1:N max = |N-side|; N-side total ⇒ exactly |N-side|
  • 1:1 max = min(m,n); both total ⇒ m=n
  • Ternary max = product of the non-“1” legs
Top traps
  • N means “any number”, not N rows.
  • Instance se cardinality sirf disprove hoti hai.
  • Ternary ≠ 3 binary (spurious tuples).
  • Relationship attribute sirf 1:1 / 1:N (N-side) mein entity mein shift ho sakta hai; M:N mein nahi.
  • Total participation extra table nahi banata.
